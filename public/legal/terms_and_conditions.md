## 1. VimlaJi Service Model
VimlaJi provides hourly home-help booking services. Customers book time slots, not separate fixed-output tasks. During the selected slot, the helper may assist with listed household services based on time available, safety, service scope, and practical feasibility.

Services may include sweeping and mopping, dusting, utensils, washing clothes, ironing and folding, kitchen help, and general home help, subject to slot duration and operational availability.

## 2. Service Availability
VimlaJi operates only in selected serviceable areas. Users outside active areas may explore the app but may not be able to place bookings.

VimlaJi may restrict, disable, or charge extra travel fees for certain areas. Availability of instant booking, scheduled booking, specific time slots, and helper assignment is controlled by VimlaJi operations/admin systems.

## 3. Account and Login
Customers may login through phone OTP, Google sign-in, or other supported methods. Helpers may login using helper ID and password. Admin access is restricted to authorized VimlaJi operators.

Users are responsible for keeping OTPs, passwords, phones, and account access secure. VimlaJi is not responsible for misuse caused by sharing OTPs, passwords, or device access.

## 4. Booking Rules
A booking is created or confirmed only after successful payment verification and operational acceptance according to the current workflow.

A booking may remain in placed/pending status until accepted, assigned, or confirmed by VimlaJi/admin/backend systems. VimlaJi may reject, cancel, reschedule, or refund a booking if the slot, helper, address, payment, or service condition cannot be fulfilled.

Scheduled bookings require minimum lead time as shown in the app. Near-time requests may be treated as instant bookings and may be disabled if instant service is not available.

## 5. Customer Responsibilities
Customers must:

- Provide accurate name, phone number, address, and service location.
- Ensure an adult is present at the service location during the booking.
- Provide safe, respectful, and lawful working conditions for helpers.
- Provide required cleaning tools, equipment, machines, and consumables.
- Keep valuables, cash, jewellery, personal documents, medicines, and fragile items safely stored before service starts.
- Clearly explain priority tasks at the beginning of the slot.
- Not ask helpers to perform unsafe, illegal, abusive, heavy-duty, hazardous, or out-of-scope work.
- Not directly hire, solicit, threaten, harass, or mistreat helpers.

## 6. Equipment and Consumables
VimlaJi does not provide cleaning equipment, machines, tools, detergents, chemicals, cloths, brushes, buckets, brooms, mops, ironing equipment, utensils, or other consumables unless explicitly stated in a separate written offer.

All required equipment and consumables must be arranged by the customer. The helper may refuse work that cannot be performed safely or practically due to lack of proper equipment.

## 7. What Is Not Included
Unless specifically approved by VimlaJi, services do not include:

- Deep cleaning, pest control, acid cleaning, sofa/carpet shampooing, chimney cleaning, bathroom deep stain removal, or heavy-duty cleaning.
- Moving heavy furniture, lifting heavy goods, climbing unsafe heights, electrical/plumbing repair, painting, construction work, or hazardous tasks.
- Childcare, elderly medical care, nursing, driving, cooking beyond basic kitchen help, pet handling, or personal care services.
- Any illegal, unsafe, abusive, discriminatory, or inappropriate request.
